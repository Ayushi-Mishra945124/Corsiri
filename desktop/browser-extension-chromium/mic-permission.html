Need to manually unblock?

  1. Click the 🔒 lock / tune icon on the left side of Chrome's address bar.
  2. Set Microphone from Block to Allow.
  3. On Windows 10/11: Go to Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone and ensure "Let desktop apps access your microphone" is turned ON.
Your voice audio is only processed locally for speech recognition during active recording.
